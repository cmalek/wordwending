# Mission: Old English Grammar And Translation

## Why
I want to read and translate Old English with increasing confidence, instead of depending on isolated lookup or half-remembered grammar rules. I want a working command of grammar, pronunciation, dialect cues, and historical background when they directly help me understand real Old English texts.

## Success looks like
- I can explain and use core Old English grammatical patterns while reading real passages.
- I can make plausible first translations and justify them from the form on the page.
- I can use pronunciation, dialect, and historical-linguistic cues when they genuinely clarify grammar or meaning.

## Constraints
- Keep lessons tightly scoped and quick to finish.
- Prefer examples from Old English texts and closely related reference works.
- Build reusable reference sheets so terms and patterns stay stable across sessions.

## Out of scope
- Engineering workflows for OCR, parsing, retrieval, or agent tooling
- Exhaustive Indo-European linguistics unless it directly helps reading Old English
- Broad software design topics not tied to learning the language
